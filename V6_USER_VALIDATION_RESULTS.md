## Interpretation

- The adaptive controller increasingly uses spatial multiplexing as SNR improves.
- Under Rayleigh fading, Alamouti remains the dominant mode at lower SNR because spatial diversity is more valuable.
- Under Rician fading, spatial multiplexing dominates earlier due to the more favorable channel structure.
- The V7 work package adds antenna correlation as a robustness test; the current estimator does not exploit cross-antenna covariance.
